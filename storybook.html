01 The question

Why coho died in clean-looking creeks

Adult coho returned to urban streams in numbers, spawned — or tried to — and then died before reproducing. The water was not obviously polluted in the way regulators usually test for: metals were within limits, the stream looked fine, and the fish had made it back from the ocean.

The investigation that followed narrowed the cause to stormwater itself, then to the particulate fraction of stormwater, then to a single tire-derived molecule. The research question this site is built around is the next step: what do the measurements tell us about where, when and how much?

02 The samples

Where the data came from

Sampling design determines what a dataset can and cannot say. Storm-event sampling captures peaks that grab samples miss; dry-weather sampling gives a baseline but understates exposure. Any comparison across sites has to account for that difference before it accounts for anything else.

03 The measurements

What the numbers looked like

The most useful finding in any contaminant dataset is usually the shape of the distribution rather than a single number: how many samples were non-detects, how wide the range was, and which places produced the highest values. The explorer on this site computes those summaries directly from the database so the story and the data cannot drift apart.

04 The finding

The result that changes the picture

A finding is only useful if it changes a decision. For 6PPD-quinone, the decisions live in stormwater design, monitoring programs and product regulation — so the strongest results are the ones that give one of those three a number to act on.

05 What comes next

From findings to a usable database

The natural next step for this work is an open, queryable database: a place where a student, a stormwater engineer or a regulator can ask a question of the data without emailing anyone. This static site is the front half of that — the narrative, the summaries and the interface pattern — designed so the data layer can be replaced without rewriting the pages.
